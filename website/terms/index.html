MedNova is an educational medical reasoning game built around clinical case puzzles, diagnostic practice, learning tools, statistics and online play with friends. It is run by its owner.

Its cases and game results are not a substitute for professional medical education, clinical supervision, diagnosis, treatment, or independent professional judgment. Users remain responsible for how they apply information outside the game.

MedNova is not medical advice. All cases and people in it are fictional.
