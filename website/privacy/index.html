MedNova · Privacy Policy

Privacy, in plain words.

01 · Who we are

MedNova is run by its owner, not a company.

MedNova is an educational medical reasoning game. It is run by its owner, who decides how your information is used (the "controller"). On Google Play the developer is listed as Yazan Yousif and on the App Store as Yazan Mohamed. MedNova is built and supported by a small team in Egypt. This policy explains what the app really collects, why, where it is kept and what you can do about it.

MedNova is for education only. It is not medical advice, and all cases and patients in it are fictional.

02 · Your account

An account needs your email (or Google) and a username.

Sign-up. When you create an account we receive your email address, a password and the username you choose (also used as your display name). Sign-in and email codes are handled by Supabase Auth. Your password is stored by Supabase only as a one-way hash; MedNova never sees or stores it in plain text.

Google sign-in. If you sign in with Google, Google confirms who you are and shares your email address and basic profile (name and picture) with us. We never see your Google password, and we use only your email to make your account.

Sign in with Apple. On iPhone you can sign in with Apple. Apple confirms who you are and shares your name and an email address with us; if you choose Hide My Email, we get a private relay address from Apple instead of your real one. We never see your Apple ID password. We also keep a token from Apple so that, when you delete your account, we can tell Apple to remove MedNova from the apps using your Apple ID.

Profile. Your account has a profile with your username, display name, avatar (the character you pick) and a friend code that others can use to add you.

Guests. You can play as a guest without an account. Guest progress stays on your phone and is not linked to you on our servers.

03 · Your play

We keep your games, scores and wallet.

  • Plays and scores: which games you play, your answers and results, scores, streaks and times.
  • Learning: the cases you miss and the cards you collect, so you can review them later.
  • EXP and tokens: your wallet is kept on your phone and, when you are signed in, synced to your account.
  • Sparks: your Sparks bar, what each play cost and when it refills.
  • Rewards: your daily gift days, daily missions, Grand Rounds stamps, chests and what they held, achievements and cards.
  • Style: the characters, frames, titles, win effects and stickers you own and wear, and gifts you send or receive.
  • Leaderboards and likes: your place on the boards, monthly prizes, and the wallpapers and posts you like.
  • Your cases and words: what you write in Learn, and whether it was approved.

04 · Friends and challenges

Playing with others means some of your data is shared with them.

  • Friends: friend requests, your friends list and the people you block.
  • Presence: when you were last seen in the app, shown to your friends.
  • Inbox: messages and gifts sent to you, and which you claimed.
  • Reports: if you report a drawing, we keep the report and the drawing so it can be reviewed.

Other players can see your username, avatar and scores on leaderboards and your profile. Do not use your real name as a username if you do not want it shown.

05 · On your phone

Some data stays on your device.

MedNova stores your sign-in session, settings, saved plays and wallet on your phone so the app works offline and opens quickly. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Photos. When you save a wallpaper, your phone may ask to let MedNova add photos. MedNova only adds the picture you save; it never reads your gallery.

06 · Ads

Ads are your choice: you watch one only to earn a reward.

MedNova shows rewarded ads only when you tap "Watch an ad" (for Sparks or a token). Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to stop fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your rough location from your internet address, and how you interact with the ad. We send Google your account id with each rewarded ad only so Google can tell our server you earned the reward.

Your choice. In the EU, the UK and some US states, the app asks for your consent before any personalised ads, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. On iPhone, the app asks whether it may track you across apps; you can change this in iPhone Settings > Privacy > Tracking. If you say no, you may still see ads that are not personalised. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Google's use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

07 · Crash reports and stats

We see crashes and which pages are used, not who you are.

MedNova uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to receive a report when the app crashes (the error, the phone model and system version, the app version and your account id, so we can find and fix the problem), and Google Analytics for Firebase to count which pages are opened and which games are started and finished. These stats include an app-instance id and device details, but not your name, email or anything you type, and the advertising features of Analytics are turned off.

08 · Notifications

Notifications are off until you say yes.

If you allow notifications, your phone gives us a push address, which we keep with your account to send friend requests, game invites, Inbox gifts and at most one reminder a day (your daily reward, a full Sparks bar, or Grand Rounds closing). They are delivered through Expo's push service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging or Apple Push Notification service. Turn either kind off in Settings, or turn all of them off in your phone's settings. Signing out removes the push address.

09 · Purchases

Payments go through Google Play or the App Store.

Token packs and MedNova Unlimited are bought through Google Play or the App Store, which handle the payment. We never see your card or bank details. We receive a record of what you bought and when (through RevenueCat, our purchase service), linked to your account id, so we can add your tokens or Unlimited. The store also keeps its own record under its own privacy policy.

10 · Where it is kept

Our servers are in the EU, in Frankfurt.

Account and game data is stored with Supabase, which acts as our processor and hosts it on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Supabase handles it only on our instructions. If you use MedNova from outside the EU, your data travels to these servers.

Other services that handle some data for us, each only for the job described above: Google (AdMob ads, Firebase crash reports, stats and notifications), Expo (delivering notifications), Apple and Google (purchases and notifications) and RevenueCat (purchase records). Some of them process data outside the EU, under their own safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

MedNova does not sell your data, and never shares your answers, scores or messages with advertisers.

11 · Why we use it

We use your data only to run the game for you.

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To save your progress, scores, wallet, Sparks and learning cards.
  • To run daily missions, Grand Rounds, chests, cards, leaderboards and their prizes.
  • To run friends and leaderboards.
  • To keep play safe: blocking, reviewing reports and stopping misuse.
  • To answer your messages and bug reports, and to review the cases and words you write.
  • To show the rewarded ads you choose to watch and pay their rewards, and to process your purchases.
  • To send the notifications you allowed.
  • To find and fix crashes and see which games and pages are used, so we can improve them.

When you write to us through Contact or the red ! button, we keep your message, where it happened and any email you give, so we can answer and fix the problem.

Legal basis. Under the EU GDPR and the data protection laws that apply to you, we use your data because it is needed to provide the service you asked for (our contract with you), for our legitimate interest in keeping MedNova safe, working and improving (crash reports and stats), and, for personalised ads and notifications, because you agreed. You can withdraw your agreement at any time in Settings.

12 · How long we keep it

Until you delete your account.

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and the rows linked to it (profile, plays, wallet, Sparks, rewards, items, misses, cards, friends, blocks, push addresses and ad reward records) are removed from our servers. Crash reports and stats are kept by Firebase for up to 14 months and are not linked to your name. Purchase records may be kept as long as tax law requires. Data on your phone stays until you delete the app.

13 · Deleting your account

You can delete it yourself, in the app.

Open Profile and tap "Delete my account", then confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and the app returns to guest mode. If you cannot open the app, write to us and we will delete it for you.

14 · Your rights

Your data is yours: you can see, fix or delete it.

  • Access: ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: ask us to fix data that is wrong.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app, or ask us to.
  • Objection: object to how we use your data, or ask us to limit it.

These rights come from the data protection laws that apply to you, including the EU and UK GDPR, the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and the Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020). To use them, write to us (see Contact). You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

15 · Age

MedNova is for people 16 or older.

You must be 16 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you think a child has made an account, write to us and we will delete it.

16 · Changes

If this policy changes, this page changes.

We update this policy when MedNova's features or data use change. The current version is always on this page.
